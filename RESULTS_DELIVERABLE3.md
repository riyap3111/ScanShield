- **Logistic regression** — Linear reference baseline: fast, fully interpretable coefficients, carried over from Deliverable 2 so the new models have a like-for-like comparator.
- **Random Forest** — Bagged trees: captures threshold interactions a linear model cannot, resists overfitting through averaging, and gives impurity and permutation importances.
- **Gradient Boosting** — Boosted trees: fits residual errors in sequence, usually the strongest family on tabular flow data, and the planned XGBoost stand-in.

### Monitoring plan

| signal                                         | threshold_or_trigger                                                                  | owner_and_response                                                                                                        | feedback_use                                                  |
|:-----------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Feature drift (PSI vs training benign traffic) | Any feature PSI > 0.25 for 2 consecutive days, or > 0.1 for 7 days                    | ML owner (Ojas) re-checks the feature against the capture pipeline, then decides retrain or roll back                     | Drifting features are the first candidates for the next refit |
| Alert volume                                   | Daily alerts outside 0.5x-3x the observed baseline (median 0/day, 90th pct 390)       | SOC duty analyst triages; a spike is either a real scan campaign or a broken sensor, and both need an answer the same day | Volume baseline is recomputed monthly                         |
| Score distribution of alerts                   | Median alert score drops below 0.90, or the share of alerts scoring 0.50-0.70 doubles | ML owner reviews calibration; borderline alerts mean the boundary has moved                                               | Triggers threshold re-selection on fresh labelled data        |
| Analyst-confirmed precision                    | Confirmed-true rate on sampled alerts falls below 90% over a rolling 2 weeks          | SOC lead and ML owner review the false alarms together                                                                    | Confirmed labels go straight into the retraining pool         |
| Missed scans found after the fact              | Any scan confirmed by another control that ScanShield did not flag                    | Treated as an incident review: the flow is replayed against the model and added to the regression set                     | Becomes a permanent test case the next model must pass        |
| Allowlist suppressions                         | Suppression share above 25% of alerts, or any entry past its expiry date              | Security team re-approves or removes the entry                                                                            | Keeps the allowlist from quietly becoming a blind spot        |
| Model freshness                                | Scheduled review every 90 days regardless of other signals                            | Both owners re-run the full pipeline on recent traffic                                                                    | Retrain on the accumulated confirmed labels                   |

|-------:|:----------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------|
|      1 | Score           | Every completed flow is scored; score, model version, feature-set version and threshold are written to the alert log                                                                        | Automatic                      |
|      2 | Allowlist check | Alerts matching an approved scanner entry on source, port, schedule and expiry are marked suppressed, never deleted                                                                         | Automatic, fully audited       |
|      3 | Tier            | Score >= 0.99 high, 0.90-0.99 medium, threshold-0.90 low; low-tier alerts are grouped by source rather than raised one by one                                                               | Automatic                      |
|      4 | Correlate       | Alerts are grouped per source host over 5 min, 1 h and 24 h windows; a source crossing 50 distinct ports with over 80% unanswered is escalated even if each flow scored low                 | Automatic (src/slow_scan.py)   |
|      5 | Human review    | An analyst confirms or rejects. No automatic blocking at any tier: a false alarm must not be able to cut off a real user                                                                    | Human decision, required       |
|      6 | Respond         | Confirmed external scan: rate-limit the source at the perimeter and open an incident. Confirmed internal scan: contact the owner and, if it is legitimate, add a time-boxed allowlist entry | Human-initiated                |
|      7 | Feed back       | Every analyst verdict is stored with the flow and the score, forming the labelled pool the next model is trained and tested on                                                              | Automatic capture, human label |

- `bash run_all.sh` rebuilds everything above from the raw CSVs (last full run: ? s).
- `random_state = 42` everywhere; the tuned configurations are recorded in `outputs/tables/model_tuning.csv`.
- The test split was scored once, after the threshold was frozen on validation.
